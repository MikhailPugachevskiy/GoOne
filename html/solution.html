Our solution is Go One

One app that combines every bike-sharing provider. Riders get an aggregated map, a live price comparison, one account, and a faster path to the nearest bike.

Four answers. One app.

After asking survey participants which features they would value, the picture was clear: overview, price, a single account, and time. Go One is built around those four.

Aggregated overview

All partner bikes appear on one map. 80% of respondents wanted this combined view.

Price comparison

Filter for the lowest fare or a favourite operator before you unlock. 73% asked for this.

One account

Pay by card or PayPal once. 93% wanted a single login across every provider.

Time saved

The nearest bike is often from another operator. Go One shows the ride that is actually closest.
