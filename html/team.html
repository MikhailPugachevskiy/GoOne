Each founder owns one management field so the company can move from business plan to MVP without splitting focus across four overlapping jobs.

Business strategy, human resources, and public representation. She frames how Go One goes to market.

Jean-Sebastian Mörschel · CMO

Brand ambassador, marketing strategy, and research. He carries the value proposition to riders and partners.

Michael Pugachevskiy · COO

Operations, culture and vision, and corporate governance. He keeps product delivery and company structure aligned.

Max Zimmermann · CFO

Corporate finance, funding and budgeting, and financial reporting. He owns the two investment rounds and the path to break-even.
