Chapter 5 · Business

Customers, competition, and the path to break-even

Go One stands on two pillars: bike-sharing providers and their riders. We raise a first round of EUR 200,000 in Q3 2019, launch the MVP at the end of the year, raise a second round in Q2 2020, and reach break-even in 2021.

Two customer pillars. A clear competitive play.

Providers supply the bikes. Riders supply the demand. Competitors already try to sit in the middle. Go One competes by shipping an MVP fast, building operator relationships, and offering one account instead of extra sign-ups.

Providers

Our target customers include Nextbike, Call a Bike, Donkey Republic, Mobike, Byke and LimeBike.

Riders

Based on the Nextbike user profile (2018): half are between 25 and 44, 71% are male and 65% are academics.

Competitors

On our competitor map, urbi comes closest. Other apps offer less functionality or cover fewer providers in Germany.

How we compete

Go to market with the MVP, foster provider partnerships, and raise usefulness with a single Go One account.

From MVP to European roll-out

The MVP launches at the end of 2019 and earns a fee per redirected user. Version 2.0 is developed during 2020. The plan assumes that Europe grows about 1.5% per month, with up to 20% market share in Germany and up to 5% in certain parts of Europe.
