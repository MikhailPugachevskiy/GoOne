Customers, competition, and the path to break-even

Go One stands on two pillars: bike-sharing providers and their riders. We enter with an MVP, grow coverage, then raise two rounds of EUR 200,000 to reach break-even in 2021.

Find your bike with Go One

Instead of jumping between apps, riders complete the whole trip in one place: scan the street, narrow the list, pick a bike, pay, and unlock.

1. Overview

All available bikes from partner providers appear in your area on one map.

2. Filters

Narrow the map by a specific operator or by the lowest price before you walk.

3. Choose

Select the bike that is closest, cheapest, or from the brand you already trust.

4. Pay

Checkout stays inside Go One. Pay by credit card or PayPal with the same account.

5. Unlock

Scan the QR code on the bike and receive a numerical lock code to start the ride.

One scan across the fleets people already use

The product is an aggregator, not another operator. Go One sits on top of the apps riders already know and routes them to the bike that is actually nearest.
